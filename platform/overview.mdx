---
title: "Partner accounts overview"
description: "Onboard your own customers as connected accounts, check their balances, and run batch payouts on their behalf."
---

Partner accounts are for vertical SaaS providers and fintech companies that offer MyGigsters to their own customers. As a partner, you onboard each customer as a connected account, hand off identity verification, and move money on their behalf.

## Key concepts

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Partner account" icon="building">
    Your top-level organisation profile. You authenticate with your registered email and a private 64-character OAuth key to receive a short-lived JWT Bearer token.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connected accounts" icon="sitemap">
    The businesses, companies, or sole traders you onboard. Each gets a unique `account_id` and its own multi-currency wallet balances.
  </Card>
  <Card title="KYC magic links" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Pre-authenticated hosted onboarding links. Your customer uploads documents and completes identity verification without seeing your API credentials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sandbox simulation" icon="flask">
    Instantly simulate a connected account's KYC approval in the demo environment, so you don't wait on manual verification while you build.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How a partner integration works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Exchange your email and OAuth key for a JWT Bearer token that lasts five minutes. Rotate your OAuth key at any time.

    [Authenticate partner](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-authenticate) · [Rotate auth key](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-rotate-auth-key)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a connected account and start KYC">
    Register your customer to get their `account_id`, then generate a magic link so they can complete onboarding themselves.

    [Create connected account](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-connected-account) · [Get KYC magic link](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-ca-magic-link)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check verification status">
    Check the account's live verification state. In the demo environment, fast-track a test account to `ACTIVE` with the simulation endpoint.

    [Get connected account status](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-ca-status) · [Simulate account status](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-ca-simulate-status)
  </Step>
  <Step title="View balances and transactions">
    Read the account's multi-currency wallet balances and its transaction ledger, with date filters and pagination.

    [Get balances](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-ca-balance) · [Get transactions](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-ca-transactions)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run batch payouts">
    Submit high-volume batch payouts over NPP or standard bank transfer, then track each line item.

    [Batch payout](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-batch-payout) · [Batch status](/api-reference/partner-account/partner-batch-status)
  </Step>
</Steps>
