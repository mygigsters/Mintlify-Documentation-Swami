<Update label="October 2026" tags={["EarlyPay"]}>
  ## EarlyPay
  - Choose financing as an option when paying freelancer invoices
  - Due dates on each deployment
  - EarlyPay amounts are now calculated from updated invoice values
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" tags={["EarlyPay"]}>
  ## EarlyPay
  - Rate configuration, available through the API
  - Separate treatment for new and returning users
  - Pay Now for approved freelancer invoices, with the payment recorded in the audit trail
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" tags={["Payments", "EarlyPay"]}>
  ## Payments
  - Email receipts, with content your admins can edit
  - Wallet numbers shown next to wallet names

  ## EarlyPay
  - Added a safeguard against paying a financed invoice twice
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" tags={["Payments", "Super", "EarlyPay"]}>
  ## Payments
  - Role-based access control
  - API key management
  - Admin dashboard

  ## Super
  - Webhooks for clearing house onboarding
  - Bulk upload accepts incomplete records, with freelancers finishing onboarding later through a magic link
  - ABNs are validated against the Australian Business Register when you onboard or update an employer
  - Payment history timeline showing the payment and fund stages

  ## EarlyPay
  - Auto EarlyPay
  - One-off and automatic EarlyPay flows for freelancers
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" tags={["EarlyPay"]}>
  ## EarlyPay
  - One-off EarlyPay
  - Introductory EarlyPay offer
  - Invoice financing: capital requests, deployments, ledger, spot repayments, and fee invoices
</Update>

<Update label="May 2026" tags={["Super"]}>
  ## Super
  - Magic-link onboarding for employers and freelancers
  - Bulk upload of freelancers
  - Embeddable onboarding components for your own product
  - Onboarding status tracking for admins
</Update>

<Update label="April 2026" tags={["Super", "EarlyPay"]}>
  ## Super
  - Field-level encryption for tax file numbers
  - Uniqueness checks for member numbers and ABNs during onboarding

  ## EarlyPay
  - EarlyPay ledger with summary cards, currency breakdown, and a transactions table
</Update>

<Update label="March 2026" tags={["Payments"]}>
  ## Payments
  - Instant payouts
  - Beneficiary setup, with the option to view and edit beneficiary details
  - Cash balances by currency
  - Redesigned payment dashboard
</Update>

<Update label="January 2026" description="Super" tags={["Super"]}>
  ## Super
  - Employer onboarding
  - Stricter validation of ABNs, member numbers, dates of birth, and addresses on employee records
  - Fixed combined filters on employee search, and empty results from contribution history
</Update>

<Update label="January 2026" description="Version 1.2" tags={["Payments", "Current"]}>
  ## Added
  - snake_case field names across all requests and responses (breaking; requires the 1.2 header)
  - Idempotency key support on `POST` and `PUT`
  - Card SDK and Beneficiary SDK
  - Document verification, Partner Account, Wallet balances, and Credit Notes
  - Restricted API keys

  ## Changed
  - One error format for all 4xx and 5xx responses
  - HMAC-SHA256 signatures on webhooks
</Update>

<Update label="June 2025" description="Version 1.1" tags={["Payments", "Maintenance"]}>
  First release: payments, customers, worker payouts, invoicing, API keys, and webhooks. End of life 31 December 2026.
</Update>
