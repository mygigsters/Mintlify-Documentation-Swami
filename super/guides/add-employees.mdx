An employee record holds a freelancer's personal details and their super fund. You need one for each freelancer before you submit contributions for them.

## Choose how to add employees

  <Card title="Use the API" icon="code" href="/super/api-reference/employees/create-employee">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Use ready-made screens" icon="window-maximize" href="/super/components/employee-onboarding">
    Send each freelancer a magic link. They enter their own details, and the form finds their super fund for them.

</Columns>

## Add employees with the API

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the freelancer's fund">
    Pre-fill fund details with [SuperSelector](/super/api-reference/funds/superselector), or let the freelancer choose from [active superannuation funds](/super/api-reference/funds/get-active-superannuation-funds). Check a USI with [Validate superannuation fund](/super/api-reference/funds/validate-superannuation-fund).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the employee">
    Call [Create employee](/super/api-reference/employees/create-employee). To have the fund confirm it will accept contributions for this member, use [Create employee (v2)](/super/api-reference/employees/create-employee-v2) instead, which runs [member verification](/super/guides/member-verification) automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add many at once">
    Upload a CSV with [Bulk upload employees (v2)](/super/api-reference/employees/bulk-upload-employees-v2).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find and update records">
    Use [List all employees](/super/api-reference/employees/list-all-employees), [Search employees](/super/api-reference/employees/search-employees), and [Update employee](/super/api-reference/employees/update-employee).
  </Step>
</Steps>
