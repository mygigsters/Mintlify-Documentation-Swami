---
title: "Onboard an employer"
description: "Create the employer of record before you submit super contributions, through the API or with ready-made screens."
---

Every super contribution is debited from an employer. Before you can submit contributions, onboard that employer with their company details, a contact, and the bank account to debit.

## Choose how to onboard

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use the API" icon="code" href="/super/api-reference/employers/onboard-employer">
    Create the employer from your own backend. Use this when you already hold the employer's details or want your own screens.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use ready-made screens" icon="window-maximize" href="/super/components/employer-onboarding">
    Send the employer a magic link. They complete a five-step form embedded in your product.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Onboard with the API

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the employer">
    Call [Onboard employer](/super/api-reference/employers/onboard-employer) with the company details, the contact person, and the payment account. The direct debit agreement must be accepted to complete onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it was created">
    Fetch the record with [Get employer by ID](/super/api-reference/employers/get-employer-by-id), or list every employer on your account with [List client employers](/super/api-reference/employers/list-client-employers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep it up to date">
    Change details later with [Update employer](/super/api-reference/employers/update-employer).
  </Step>
</Steps>
