The management dashboard is the main screen your admin users work in. `SuperManagementEmbed` has two tabs. `SuperApp` adds a third for payments.

## Talent tab

Available in both components. It lists every invited freelancer with their onboarding status.

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| Search | Filter by name or email in real time |
| Filter by status | Complete, Pending, Not Started, Link Expired |
| Filter by date range | Narrow results by invite date |
| Send invite | Opens a dialog to send a magic-link email |
| Send reminder | Re-sends the email to freelancers who haven't finished |
| View details | Name, email, phone, address, ABN, TFN, super fund, member number, agreements |
| Export CSV | Downloads the full list |
| Bulk upload | Upload a CSV to onboard many freelancers at once |

## Clients tab

Available in both components. It has the same layout and actions as the Talent tab, for employer records. The details panel shows:

- company name, ABN, and ACN
- website and Withholding Payer Number
- contact name, title, email, and phone
- business address
- direct debit account (last 4 digits)
- agreements accepted

## Payments tab

Available in `SuperApp` only. It shows the contribution payment history across all freelancers and employers. Admins can filter by payment status, export to CSV, and page through the history.

## Bulk upload

Onboard many freelancers at once from the Talent tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the CSV template">
    The template has every supported column: first name, last name, email, phone, ABN, TFN, gender, date of birth, address, super fund USI, and member number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the data">
    Any fields left blank are treated as incomplete. Those freelancers receive an email invite to complete their details online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Drag and drop the CSV or use the file picker. Results appear immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The upload sorts freelancers into three groups:

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| All required fields provided | Fully onboarded straight away. Nothing more to do. |
| Some fields missing | The record is saved in a partial state. Send a magic link so the freelancer can finish. |
| Could not be saved | Duplicate email or missing fields that can't be recovered. Correct the row and upload again. |

## Magic links

When you send an invite from the dashboard, or through bulk upload, the freelancer or employer receives a unique, single-use link:

```text
https://yourapp.com/super/super-setup?token=abc123&onboardingFor=Talent&clientName=YourCompany
```

The component detects the token, validates the link, and launches the right onboarding form with the email pre-filled. If the link has expired or been used, it shows a clear error with the option to request a new one.

<Note>
  No code is needed on your side, as long as `frontendBaseUrl` is set to the URL where the component is mounted. The `clientName` parameter sets the support email shown on error screens.
</Note>
