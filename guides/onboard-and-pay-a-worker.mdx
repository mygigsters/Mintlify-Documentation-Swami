---
title: "Onboard and pay out a worker"
description: "Register a worker, collect their bank details, and send a payout."
---

Register a worker, collect their bank details, then pay them. Workers can be Australian (BSB) or Indian (IFSC/UPI) beneficiaries.

<Steps>

<Step title="Create the worker">

Register the worker with [Worker Create](/api-reference/workers/worker-create). You can include bank details up front, or add them later. Keep the returned `worker_id`.

</Step>

<Step title="Collect bank details — three options">

- **You already have them:** submit via [Add Bank Details](/api-reference/workers/add-bank-details) (BSB for AU, IFSC/UPI for India).
- **Hosted form:** request a [Beneficiary URL](/api-reference/workers/beneficiary-url) and redirect the worker to a secure MyGigsters-hosted page. They enter details themselves and are returned to your `return_url`.
- **Embedded form:** mount the [Beneficiary SDK](/sdks/beneficiary-sdk) directly in your web app for a fully in-context experience.

</Step>

<Step title="Send the payout">

```bash
curl -X POST https://qa-payments.mygigsters.com.au/api/v1/worker-bank-payout \
  -u "YOUR_UUID:YOUR_SECRET" \
  -H "idempotency-Key: mx174dc0-281b-4cd3-847e-d3791a84f3ab" \
  -H "X-API-Version: 1.2" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "worker_id": "wkr_1042",
    "full_name": "Ravi Kumar",
    "amount": 320.50,
    "job_id": "job_8842",
    "reference": "Week 27 payout"
  }'
```

For Indian beneficiaries you can choose the rail with `mode`: IMPS (₹1–₹5,00,000), NEFT (₹1+, no cap), RTGS (min ₹2,00,000), UPI (₹1–₹1,00,000).

</Step>

<Step title="Track the transfer">

Use the `transfer_id` from the payout response with [Payout Status](/api-reference/payouts/payout-status), or listen for `payout.succeeded` webhook events.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
  **One-off payouts:** to pay someone you haven’t registered as a worker, use [Talent Payouts](/api-reference/payouts/talent-payouts) with their bank details inline.
</Note>
