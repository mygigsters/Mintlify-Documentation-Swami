---
title: "Set up subscriptions"
description: "Bill customers on a recurring schedule."
---

Bill a customer’s default card on a recurring schedule.

<Steps>

<Step title="Prerequisite: a customer with a default card">

Subscriptions charge the customer’s saved default card — see [Charge a customer](/guides/charge-a-customer) for card setup.

</Step>

<Step title="Create the subscription">

Call [Create Subscription](/api-reference/subscriptions/create-subscription) with the customer, amount, currency, frequency and start date.

</Step>

<Step title="Manage its lifecycle">

[Update](/api-reference/subscriptions/update-subscription) to modify details, [Pause](/api-reference/subscriptions/pause-subscription) to temporarily stop billing, [Resume](/api-reference/subscriptions/resume-subscription) to continue, [Cancel](/api-reference/subscriptions/cancel-subscription) to end permanently. [List Subscriptions](/api-reference/subscriptions/list-subscriptions) shows everything on your account.

</Step>

</Steps>
