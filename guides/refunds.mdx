---
title: "Issue refunds"
description: "Refund a payment and track the refund."
---

Refund all or part of a captured payment using the original payment intent.

<Steps>

<Step title="Create the refund">

Call [Create Refund](/api-reference/payments/create-refund) with the `payment_intent_id` and the `amount` to return. Partial refunds are supported by passing an amount smaller than the original payment.

</Step>

<Step title="Track it">

Refunds progress through `RECEIVED` → `PROCESSING` → `COMPLETED` (or `FAILED`). Poll [Refund Status](/api-reference/payments/refund-status) with the `refund_id`, or use webhooks.

</Step>

</Steps>
