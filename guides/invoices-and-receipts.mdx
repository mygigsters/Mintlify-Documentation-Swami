Create branded PDF invoices with optional embedded payment links, email them to customers, and send payment/payout receipts for completed transactions.

## Invoices

- [Create and Send Invoice](/api-reference/invoices/create-send-invoice) — builds the PDF, optionally emails the customer (`send_email`) and embeds a hosted payment link (`payment_link`). GST is calculated when `gst_applicable` is true.
- [Get All Invoices](/api-reference/invoices/get-all-invoices) — list with search, date filters and pagination. Includes Xero sync status.
- [Delete Invoice](/api-reference/invoices/delete-invoice) — remove an invoice by id.

## Receipts (Coming Soon)

Receipts are transactional emails for delivery-style jobs:

- [Payment Receipt](/api-reference/invoices/payment-receipt) — emailed to the customer after they pay (delivery details, discounts, GST, card last digits).
- [Payout Receipt](/api-reference/invoices/payout-receipt) — emailed to the worker/driver after a payout (earnings, bank details).
